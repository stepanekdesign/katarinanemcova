Analysis of Real Estate Investment & Technical Potential

Every property presents opportunities.
Every property also has its constraints and risks.

Before purchasing, renovating, or initiating a development project, it is essential to understand not only the current state of a property, but also its true potential, future use options, and the factors that may influence its long-term value and total costs.


The investment and technical potential analysis combines architectural, technical, regulatory, and investment perspectives into a single expert assessment. This delivers a comprehensive 360° view of the property and creates a solid foundation for sound investment decisions.

What you need to know before deciding

  • Is the property truly suitable for your intended purpose?
  • What can you realistically build or realize—and what is unfeasible?
  • What restrictions could escalate costs or make the project impossible?
  • Where are the hidden technical, regulatory, or zoning risks?
  • Is there a way to enhance the property’s potential and market value?
  • What must be verified before buying or investing?
  • What is the recommended next course of action?

Sloped Vineyard Plot
Near Brno

Assignment & Objective

An investor was considering purchasing an attractive building plot on a vineyard slope near Brno. The location offered exceptional views, a unique natural setting, and high potential for building a family residence. The goal of the analysis was to verify whether the plot truly matched the planned investment intent and whether technical or regulatory constraints could affect future development.

Analysis Process

The assessment included an architectural study that confirmed the plot’s viability and spatial potential for the planned construction. Crucially, the analysis identified potential geological risks associated with the steep slope. Based on this recommendation, an expert geological survey and long-term slope stability measurements were carried out before finalizing the purchase. The findings subsequently confirmed active landslide movements, representing substantial technical and financial risks for future building works.

Result

Based on the expert findings, the investor decided to withdraw from the acquisition, thereby avoiding long-term technical complications and financial losses in the millions.
