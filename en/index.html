Real Estate Investment & Technical Potential Analysis

Every property offers opportunities.
Every property also has its limitations and risks.

Before purchasing, renovating or starting a development project, it is important to understand not only the current condition of a property, but also its true potential, possible future uses and the factors that may affect its future value and overall costs.


Investment and Technical Property Potential Analysis combines architectural, technical, regulatory and investment perspectives into one professional analysis. This provides a comprehensive view of the property and creates a sound basis for making the right investment decision.

What you need to know before making a decision

  • Is the property truly suitable for your intended project?
  • What can you develop on the property – and what not?
  • What limitations could increase the cost of your project or even make it impossible?
  • Where are the hidden technical, regulatory or other risks?
  • Is there a way to increase the property's potential and value?
  • What needs to be verified before purchasing or investing?
  • What next steps do I recommend?

Sloping Vineyard Plot near Brno

Brief

An investor was considering the purchase of an attractive building plot on a vineyard slope near Brno. The location offered exceptional views, a unique natural setting and significant potential for the construction of a family house.

The purpose of the analysis was to assess whether the plot was genuinely suitable for the intended investment project and whether there were any technical or other constraints that could affect the future use of the property.

Analysis Process

The assessment included an architectural study to evaluate the development potential of the plot and confirm its suitability for the intended construction.

At the same time, the analysis identified a potential geological risk associated with the plot's location on a steep slope.

Based on this recommendation, a specialist geological assessment and long-term slope stability monitoring were carried out before the purchase was completed.

The results subsequently confirmed active landslide movements, representing a significant technical and financial risk for the future development.

Result

Based on the expert findings, the investor decided not to proceed with the purchase, thereby avoiding a long-term technical and financial risk.
